About me

I am a Software Quality Manager in Jakarta. I started as a Java and TIBCO engineer building telecom provisioning systems, then moved into QA at Samsung R&D, MatahariMall and Blibli.com.

That engineering background is why I lead quality hands-on. I can review a framework design, read a pipeline and challenge a release plan, and I also run the people side: 1:1s, KPI reviews and career paths for SDETs. In 2015 I represented Indonesia at the Samsung Software Engineer Workshop in Suwon, Korea.

How I work

  • Quality is a business function. The QA roadmap follows product and operational KPIs, not just test counts.
  • Gates over heroics. Reviews such as Change Request Forms, UAT scenario sign-off and CI/CD checks beat last-minute firefighting.
  • Automate where it pays back. Frameworks, nightly regression and continuous testing so manual effort goes to judgment work.
  • Grow the team. Technical coaching and clear KPIs so SDETs advance and quality ownership spreads across squads.

Learning now

Applying generative AI to software testing. In 2026 I completed LinkedIn courses on AI for Software Testers, Generative AI and Effective Technical Communication.
